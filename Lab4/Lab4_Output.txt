=== USER_STORIES ===
As an Agile Product Owner, I have translated the Functional Requirements (FRs), Non-Functional Requirements (NFRs), and Stakeholder Goals of the **LIC Market-Driven System (MDS)** into production-ready User Stories. 

These stories are structured using the classic **"Card, Conversation, Confirmation"** model, clearly defining the **Front of the Card** (User Story & Gherkin Acceptance Criteria) and the **Back of the Card** (Technical details, non-functional constraints, and validation strategies) to ensure they are ready for Sprint Planning and development.

## Epic 1: Competitor Benchmarking & Data Management (CBM)

### User Story 1: Competitor Pricing & Feature Data Ingestion
* **ID:** `US-MDS-CBM-01`
* **Epic/Module:** Competitor Benchmarking & Data Management (CBM)

#### **FRONT OF THE CARD**
**User Story:**
> **As a** Policy Administrator,  
> **I want to** automatically ingest and manually update competitor product structures, discount rates, and coverage terms,  
> **So that** our system continuously evaluates market-competitive gaps against top rival offerings based on up-to-date baseline datasets.

**Acceptance Criteria (Gherkin):**
* **Scenario 1: Automated API/JSON Data Ingestion Validation**
  * **Given** a competitor pricing XML/JSON data stream is triggered by a scheduled cron job,
  * **When** the system parses the competitor's multi-policy discount slabs and premium structures,
  * **Then** it must validate schema compliance and set the system record status to `ACTIVE` with a timestamp.
* **Scenario 2: Handling Outdated Competitor Data**
  * **Given** active competitor pricing data in the benchmark database,
  * **When** a record exceeds thirty (30) days since its last verified update,
  * **Then** the system must trigger an administrative system alert and flag the competitor profile as `STALE`.

---

#### **BACK OF THE CARD**
* **Technical & Performance Notes:**
  * **NFR Alignment:** Adheres to `NFR-SEC-01` (AES-256 encryption at rest for the benchmark database).
  * **Integrations:** Requires external ETL pipelines connecting to competitive pricing aggregators or scraping interfaces.
* **Validation & Test Strategy:**
  * **Automated Unit Test:** Mock an invalid JSON payload containing empty discount schemas and assert that schema validation rejects the upload.
  * **System Alert Verification:** Manipulate database timestamp metrics to mock a >30-day stale record; verify that the system-wide event broker successfully issues an administrative notification.
* **Dependencies & Risks:**
  * **Risk:** Frequent schema modifications by external competitors may break the parsing algorithms, requiring robust API version fallback controls.

---

### User Story 2: Real-Time Dynamic Market Gap Analysis & Interactive Comparison Cards
* **ID:** `US-MDS-CBM-02`
* **Epic/Module:** Competitor Benchmarking & Data Management (CBM)

#### **FRONT OF THE CARD**
**User Story:**
> **As an** Insurance Agent,  
> **I want to** run a real-time market gap analysis and display visual comparison cards comparing LIC’s package to market rival offerings,  
> **So that** I can present a compelling, compliance-approved sales argument to prospective clients, highlighting our specific product advantages.

**Acceptance Criteria (Gherkin):**
* **Scenario 1: Calculation of Coverage & Premium Differentials**
  * **Given** a custom-built LIC package and the prospect’s demographic profile,
  * **When** the agent triggers the "Market Gap Analysis" engine,
  * **Then** the system must calculate the exact premium delta ($\Delta P = P_{\text{LIC}} - P_{\text{Competitor}}$) and auto-generate value-add recommendations (e.g., adding critical illness cover to close the gap).
* **Scenario 2: Comparison UI rendering with Regulatory Disclaimers**
  * **Given** the gap analysis calculation is completed,
  * **When** rendering the side-by-side comparison cards in the portal,
  * **Then** the UI must inject the IRDAI-mandated legal disclaimers and lock the layout against manual deletion before exporting to PDF.

---

#### **BACK OF THE CARD**
* **Technical & Performance Notes:**
  * **NFR Alignment:** Exporting the gap analysis and side-by-side PDF must take $\le 2.0$ seconds on both desktop and tablet views.
  * **UI Requirement:** Responsive card structure with highlighting features for better usability.
* **Validation & Test Strategy:**
  * **Math Engine Verification:** Inject a mock LIC package with premium ₹1,200/mo and competitor matching package with premium ₹1,100/mo; assert the calculated delta is exactly $+₹100/mo$.
  * **PDF Compliance Audit:** Open the generated PDF using testing software (e.g., PDFBox) to assert that the exact legal disclaimer matches the current IRDAI compliance script.
* **Dependencies & Risks:**
  * **Risk:** Showing incorrect competitor pricing poses legal risk. The UI must explicitly display the timestamp of the last competitive update.

## Epic 2: Customer-Driven Package Builder (CPB) & Consolidated Packaging Engine (CPE)

### User Story 3: Interactive Interactive Coverage Builder with Real-Time Sub-Second Recalculation
* **ID:** `US-MDS-CPB-01`
* **Epic/Module:** Customer-Driven Package Builder (CPB) & Consolidated Packaging Engine (CPE)

#### **FRONT OF THE CARD**
**User Story:**
> **As an** End-Customer (or Agent assisting a client),  
> **I want an** interactive workspace with smooth sliders to toggle insurance policy limits and add riders,  
> **So that** I can design a tailored multi-policy insurance bundle with instant pricing updates without waiting for page reloads.

**Acceptance Criteria (Gherkin):**
* **Scenario 1: Dynamic Multi-Policy Aggregation and Discount Selection**
  * **Given** the customer has added a term policy and a health policy to their visual tray,
  * **When** the user changes the Sum Assured sliders,
  * **Then** the core engine must aggregate the standalone policies, apply the corresponding volume discount (e.g., 5% off base rates for 2 policies), and display the net savings.
* **Scenario 2: Sub-Second Client Recalculation Latency**
  * **Given** a multi-policy bundle is open in the package builder UI,
  * **When** any slider or optional rider checkbox is changed,
  * **Then** the pricing engine must return the recalculated premium inside the browser in $\le 1.5$ seconds under load.

---

#### **BACK OF THE CARD**
* **Technical & Performance Notes:**
  * **NFR Alignment:** Strictly enforces `NFR-PERF-01` (latency of premium calculation API $\le 1.5\text{ seconds}$ at 10k concurrent users).
  * **Architecture:** Utilizes asynchronous REST API calls with microservice-level Redis caching of baseline premium matrices.
* **Validation & Test Strategy:**
  * **Load/Performance Test:** Run JMeter scripts simulating 10,000 threads adjusting Sum Assured values; measure response times to confirm the 95th percentile is under 1.5 seconds.
  * **Discount Rule Integration Test:** Create a baseline quote for Policy A (₹500) and Policy B (₹500); check that selecting both applies the correct combined discount and does not exceed the statutory limits set by IRDAI.
* **Dependencies & Risks:**
  * **Dependency:** Integration SLA with Core Banking/Legacy database lookup engines must stay within the 800ms window (`NFR-INT-01`).

## Epic 3: Automated Risk & Rules Engine (RUG)

### User Story 4: Automated Risk Assessment and Underwriting Triage Routing
* **ID:** `US-MDS-RUG-01`
* **Epic/Module:** Automated Risk & Rules Engine (RUG)

#### **FRONT OF THE CARD**
**User Story:**
> **As an** End-Customer (or Agent),  
> **I want** my custom-configured insurance package to be instantly analyzed for risk and regulatory compliance,  
> **So that** my quote is automatically approved, loaded, or routed for manual review without delays.

**Acceptance Criteria (Gherkin):**
* **Scenario 1: Auto-Approval Decision Pipeline**
  * **Given** a custom quote request has a total Sum Assured of $\le$ ₹5,000,000, the applicant is age $\le 45$, and the medical questionnaire is clean,
  * **When** the automated triage engine processes the submission,
  * **Then** the status must be set to `AUTO_APPROVED` and a payment gateway deep-link should be generated.
* **Scenario 2: Auto-Loading Rules Evaluation**
  * **Given** a policy application containing standard low-risk declarations (e.g., Controlled Hypertension),
  * **When** processed by the automated engine,
  * **Then** apply a standard risk-loading premium factor (e.g., $+10\%$ on base rate) and route the application to state `AUTO_LOADED_PENDING_CUSTOMER_ACCEPTANCE`.
* **Scenario 3: Escalation to Manual Underwriting Workspace**
  * **Given** an application with a Sum Assured of $>$ ₹5,000,000,
  * **When** submitted to the validation system,
  * **Then** immediately set the decision status to `MANUAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED` and queue it in the Underwriter Task Queue.

---

#### **BACK OF THE CARD**
* **Technical & Performance Notes:**
  * **NFR Alignment:** Compliance logging (`NFR-COMP-01`) must register all automated triage actions, variables, and decisions inside the immutable compliance audit schema.
  * **Engine Pattern:** Rule evaluation managed by an agile, decoupling business rules engine (such as Drools or standard JSON rules engine).
* **Validation & Test Strategy:**
  * **Boundary Value Automated Tests:**
    * Input Sum Assured ₹5,000,000 -> Assert outcome is `AUTO_APPROVED` (assuming other fields are clean).
    * Input Sum Assured ₹5,000,001 -> Assert outcome is `MANUAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED`.
  * **Regulatory Hard-Stop Verification:** Attempt to trigger a combination that violates the IRDAI boundary rules; verify that the system blocks quote generation and outputs a clean, regulatory-compliant validation warning.
* **Dependencies & Risks:**
  * **Critical Dependency:** Requires reliable uptime from internal medical classification APIs.

## Epic 4: Manual Underwriting Workdesk & Workflow (UWW)

### User Story 5: Role-Based Underwriter Workdesk Task Routing
* **ID:** `US-MDS-UWW-01`
* **Epic/Module:** Manual Underwriting Workdesk & Workflow (UWW)

#### **FRONT OF THE CARD**
**User Story:**
> **As an** Underwriter,  
> **I want to** access a prioritized queue containing manual review requests, matching my delegation authority levels,  
> **So that** I can process custom high-risk packages in order of risk level and queue priority without manual coordination bottlenecks.

**Acceptance Criteria (Gherkin):**
* **Scenario 1: Delegation Authority Access Boundary**
  * **Given** a Level 1 Underwriter is logged in,
  * **When** they view the active task queue,
  * **Then** they must only view packages with a Sum Assured of $\le$ ₹20,000,000.
* **Scenario 2: Auto-Routing based on SLA & Priority Severity**
  * **Given** multiple tasks exist in the task queue,
  * **When** the workdesk dashboard renders,
  * **Then** tasks must be sorted using an allocation score based on chronological submission age and risk metrics.

---

#### **BACK OF THE CARD**
* **Technical & Performance Notes:**
  * **NFR Alignment:** Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) must be implemented on the task queue backend controller (`NFR-SEC-01`).
  * **Integrations:** Synchronizes with Active Directory or Identity Managers (IDM) to retrieve and verify underwriter authorization groups.
* **Validation & Test Strategy:**
  * **RBAC Penetration Test:** Attempt to query a Level 2 task endpoint using a Level 1 Underwriter's authentication token; assert that a `403 Forbidden` error is returned.
  * **Sorting Rule Validation:** Populate the database with three review tickets (Ticket A: 48 hours old, low risk; Ticket B: 2 hours old, high risk; Ticket C: 1 hour old, senior-level risk). Verify queue priority sorting logic.

---

  * **Then** the UI must dynamically calculate the revised total package premium and breakdown of component premiums.

---

#### **BACK OF THE CARD**

### User Story 7: Overrides, Escalation Rules, and Compliance Audit Trail
* **ID:** `US-MDS-UWW-03`
* **Epic/Module:** Manual Underwriting Workdesk & Workflow (UWW)

#### **FRONT OF THE CARD**
**User Story:**
> **As a** Senior/Chief Underwriter,  
> **I want to** receive automated escalation notices for review cases where standard risk thresholds are overridden,  
> **So that** high-impact manual underwriting modifications undergo secondary review and are written to an unalterable compliance audit trail.

**Acceptance Criteria (Gherkin):**
* **Scenario 1: Triggering Secondary Approval Workflow**
  * **Given** a Level 2 underwriter triggers a manual policy risk override,
  * **When** the financial impact of the override exceeds ₹50,000 in annual premium difference,
  * **Then** block immediate approval and route the task to the Chief Underwriter's approval desk, flagging the status as `PENDING_SECONDARY_OVERRIDE_APPROVAL`.
* **Scenario 2: Immutable Auditing Requirements**
  * **Given** an underwriter confirms an override or approval decision,
  * **When** the decision is saved to the compliance engine,
  * **Then** the database must write a non-updatable log entry containing: User ID, Timestamp, Initial Premium parameters, Modified Premium parameters, and Text Justification.

---

#### **BACK OF THE CARD**
* **Technical & Performance Notes:**
  * **NFR Alignment:** Immutability compliance baseline requires a minimum of 10 years of audit retention (`NFR-COMP-01`).
  * **Persistence Architecture:** Audit logging framework writes to a read-heavy, write-once partition with column-level write protection.
* **Validation & Test Strategy:**
  * **Override Level Integration Test:** Mock an override generating a difference of ₹51,000 and confirm secondary approval is triggered. Mock an override generating a difference of ₹49,000 and confirm manual approval completes immediately.
  * **Database Security Check:** Execute SQL updates on existing log tables as root; confirm that database security privileges block all editing/deleting operations on compliance log tables.

=== SPRINT_PLAN ===
As a Scrum Master, I have analyzed the dependencies, technical risks, and business value of the 8 user stories for the **LIC Market-Driven System (MDS)**. 

To deliver a high-value, functional product incrementally, I have organized these user stories into **3 logical, highly cohesive Sprints**. This progression ensures that we first build our foundational calculation and rules engines, next construct the back-office compliance and manual review screens, and finally roll out the front-end agent enablement and benchmarking modules.

---

### Sprint Roadmap at a Glance

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ SPRINT 1: The Core Calculation Engine    │
│ (Build Packages, Calc Rates, Route Risk) │
└────────────────────┬─────────────────────┘
                     ▼
┌──────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ SPRINT 2: Operational Control Workspace  │
│ (Queue Routing, Underwriting & Audits)   │
└────────────────────┬─────────────────────┘
                     ▼
┌──────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ SPRINT 3: Commercial Sales & Insights    │
│ (Mobile Sales, Commissions & Benchmarks) │
└──────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### **Sprint 1: Core Calculation Engine & Automated Risk Routing**
* **Sprint Goal:** Establish the core calculation infrastructure, ingest baseline competitor data, and execute automated underwriting triage rules to prove the system can price and route risks automatically.

#### **Included User Stories:**
1. **`US-MDS-CPB-01`**: Interactive Coverage Builder with Real-Time Sub-Second Recalculation
   * *Focus:* Building the core pricing engine, sliders, and multi-policy volume discounts.
2. **`US-MDS-CBM-01`**: Competitor Pricing & Feature Data Ingestion
   * *Focus:* Building the ETL pipelines to load competitor baseline data (required early to establish comparison matrices).
3. **`US-MDS-RUG-01`**: Automated Risk Assessment and Underwriting Triage Routing
   * *Focus:* The automated business rules engine (e.g., Drools) to auto-approve, load, or escalate quotes instantly.

#### **Scrum Master Rationale & Dependencies:**
* **Technical Dependency:** `US-MDS-CPB-01` is the ultimate prerequisite for the entire system. We cannot assess risk (`RUG-01`) or sell policies (`ASP-01`) until the engine can accurately calculate a multi-policy premium bundle in sub-second times.
* **Functional Dependency:** `US-MDS-CBM-01` is scheduled here to ensure that when we build the rules engine and comparison systems, our databases are already populated with structured competitor baseline schemas.
* **Business Value:** At the end of Sprint 1, we achieve a **Minimum Viable Product (MVP)** pipeline: an end-user can build a custom package, and the system can instantly price it and decide if it is auto-approved or requires human intervention.

---

### **Sprint 2: Manual Underwriting & Operational Control Workspace**
* **Sprint Goal:** Build a secure, role-based, and auditable workdesk for human underwriters to process and adjust high-risk cases routed by the Sprint 1 rules engine.

#### **Included User Stories:**
1. **`US-MDS-UWW-01`**: Role-Based Underwriter Workdesk Task Routing
   * *Focus:* Queuing, sorting logic, and Active Directory RBAC security boundaries based on underwriter authority levels.
2. **`US-MDS-UWW-02`**: Interactive Evaluation & Manual Risk Loading Factor Entry
   * *Focus:* The underwriter UI, side-by-side medical document viewer, and manual rate-loading adjustments.
3. **`US-MDS-UWW-03`**: Overrides, Escalation Rules, and Compliance Audit Trail
   * *Focus:* Strict security, secondary manager approvals for major overrides, and immutable compliance database logging.

#### **Scrum Master Rationale & Dependencies:**
* **Technical Dependency:** These stories rely directly on Sprint 1’s automated triage pipeline (`US-MDS-RUG-01`). We cannot route tasks to an underwriter’s desk until the automated rules engine has the capability to assign tasks a `MANUAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED` state.
* **Risk Mitigation:** Grouping the entire Underwriting Workdesk (`UWW-01`, `02`, `03`) in a single sprint ensures that the core UX, calculation math (loading factors), and compliance auditing are developed and tested simultaneously, mitigating the legal risk of unlogged manual premium overrides.
* **Business Value:** Internal operations are fully unlocked. LIC underwriter staff can securely process complex, high-value custom packages with complete regulatory peace of mind.

---

### **Sprint 3: Commercial Sales Enablement & Competitive Insights**
* **Sprint Goal:** Empower the agency force with mobile-optimized sales tools, instant commission transparency, and real-time competitor comparison gap analyses to drive conversions in the field.

#### **Included User Stories:**
1. **`US-MDS-ASP-01`**: Agent Sales Portal, Instant Commission Transparency & Quote Generation
   * *Focus:* Mobile-friendly sales UI, instant tier-based commission calculations, and PDF quote export/sharing via SMS/WhatsApp.
2. **`US-MDS-CBM-02`**: Real-Time Dynamic Market Gap Analysis & Interactive Comparison Cards
   * *Focus:* Real-time comparison math ($\Delta P$), IRDAI compliance disclaimers, and interactive, exportable comparison cards.

#### **Scrum Master Rationale & Dependencies:**
* **Technical Dependency:** The Agent Sales Portal (`ASP-01`) and Gap Analysis (`CBM-02`) require a fully mature, stable premium calculation engine (`US-MDS-CPB-01`, Sprint 1) and a complete competitor data pipeline (`US-MDS-CBM-01`, Sprint 1). Attempting to build sales dashboards or gap-comparison UI cards before the calculation microservices and competitor schemas are mature would result in extensive code rewrite and throwaway work.
* **Business Value:** This sprint delivers the "wow factor" and external commercial value. Agents can walk up to prospective clients, input custom metrics, prove LIC's superiority via interactive side-by-side comparison cards, and instantly close the sale via their mobile devices.
